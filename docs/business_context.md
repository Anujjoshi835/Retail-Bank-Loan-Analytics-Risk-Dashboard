# Business Context

A retail bank approves loans for people with very different incomes, jobs and credit histories. A loan that goes into default costs the bank the unpaid balance, so the lending team needs to know which kinds of borrowers carry the most risk and which kinds of loans make up the book.

## Aim of the project

Give the lending and risk teams a single place to:

- see how big the loan book is and what it is made of;
- compare default rates across borrower groups;
- check the effect of income, credit score, age, employment, co-signers and mortgages;
- filter all of the above interactively.

## Expected value

The default rate tables point to the borrower segments that need tighter approval rules or closer monitoring, and the portfolio pages show where the bank's lending is concentrated.
